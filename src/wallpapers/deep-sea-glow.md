Deep Sea Glow is a moody, deep-blue gradient built specifically for tall phone screens — it reads as an almost-black navy at the top of the display and warms slightly toward the bottom, so status bar icons and widgets stay legible without any extra tinting.

It's tuned for AMOLED and OLED panels: the deep tones stay rich without crushing to pure black, and the file is checked at native phone resolution so lock-screen clock and notification text never overlaps a busy area of the image.

Comes in three portrait crops — 1440×3200 native, 1170×2532 for iPhone, and 1080×1920 for older Full HD phones — so it sits correctly behind your lock screen and home screen without any cropping guesswork.
