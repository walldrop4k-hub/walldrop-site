Cyber Alley is a rain-slicked, neon-lit side street built for desktops that want some energy on screen without going full RGB. Violet and cyan light pools on wet pavement while the upper half of the frame stays dark enough that window title bars and top-docked taskbars never fight the image for attention.

It's tuned for wide gaming monitors — the neon signage sits off to the sides rather than dead center, so a centered clock widget or a row of desktop icons down the middle never overlaps the busiest part of the composition.

At 2K the file is checked for compression artifacts in the darker shadow areas, which is usually where a lower-quality neon wallpaper falls apart first.
