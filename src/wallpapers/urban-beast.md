

Urban Beast is an aggressive mobile wallpaper with a mechanical edge. A low, ultra-wide black car with a widebody kit sits in the middle of a city street, seen from directly behind, its twin chrome exhaust pipes gleaming and a prancing-horse badge visible on the rear. Crosswalk stripes lead toward the car, and tall buildings with overhead cables frame the pale sky. The muted, grey-blue palette makes the metallic finish stand out. It suits automotive enthusiasts, tuning fans, and anyone who likes cyberpunk-style street scenes. The rear view gives the image a clear focal point even on a small screen. At 3000 × 6667 pixels, this is a very tall mobile wallpaper, so it has plenty of detail for the exhaust and the wheel arches on a modern phone.
