Radiant Koi is a dreamlike mobile wallpaper built around an underwater scene. Beams of that light spill down into a deep, dark void, where colorful koi fish swim in a loose, drifting stream. It suits fans of aquatic art, surreal imagery, and calm wallpapers that feel like a quiet moment. The tall layout makes the koi and light beams feel like they flow down the screen.
