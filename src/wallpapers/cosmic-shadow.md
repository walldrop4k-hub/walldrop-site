Cosmic Shadow is a dark, monochrome desktop wallpaper that pulls you into deep space. A curly-haired figure in black and white reaches toward the viewer out of the darkness, with glowing white eyes shining through the shadow. Smoke and stardust swirl around the arm and shoulder, and shooting comets streak across the frame, adding motion without adding color. Because the palette is almost entirely black and white, it works well on a minimal or dark-mode desktop, where bright wallpapers would feel out of place. The near-black background also suits AMOLED and OLED screens. At 2752 × 1536 pixels, this is a sharp desktop wallpaper for a standard widescreen monitor, with enough detail in the smoke and stars to reward a closer look. It suits fans of surreal, cosmic art who want a mysterious, slightly eerie mood rather than a bright, cheerful one.
