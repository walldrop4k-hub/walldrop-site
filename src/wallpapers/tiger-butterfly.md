

Tiger Butterfly is a striking mobile wallpaper that pairs power with calm. A tiger in profile looks up at a monarch butterfly that has settled just in front of its nose, its orange wings catching the light against a pitch-black background. The tiger's stripes and whiskers are sharply drawn, and the butterfly adds a soft, gentle counterpoint to the big cat's strength. The black background is AMOLED-friendly, so the image looks rich on OLED screens, including Samsung phones, and the dark areas blend into the screen's edges. The tall layout places the tiger low on the right and leaves the upper half of the screen as open black space for the clock. It suits wildlife fans and anyone who likes calm, nature-driven artwork. At 1242 × 2688 pixels, it's a mobile-sized wallpaper that works well on most modern phones without needing to be scaled up.
