Anime Eyes is a dark, moody desktop wallpaper built around one striking image. Brown anime eyes peer through a slit in a sheet of torn crimson paper, and everything else falls away into a pitch-black background. The composition is simple and centered, so it sits behind desktop icons without competing with them. The near-black areas suit OLED monitors too, where true black stays dark and the red paper edges stand out. It's a good pick for anime fans who want something more atmospheric than bright, busy artwork, especially if your desktop already uses a dark theme. This version is 1186 × 667 pixels, so it works best on smaller screens. On a large monitor it will need to be scaled up, so look for a bigger version if sharpness matters to you. The overall mood is quiet and a little mysterious, like someone watching from behind a torn page.
