Pastel Dawn is a soft pink-to-lavender gradient built specifically for a phone lock screen — light enough at the top of the display that white status-bar text and the clock stay easy to read, with a little more color depth lower down where notification widgets tend to sit.

The gradient is smooth enough to hold up on a tall, dense display without banding, and it's checked at native iPhone resolution so nothing gets stretched or cropped oddly around the notch or dynamic island.

It pairs well with light-colored app icons and looks calmer than most of the high-saturation gradients you'll find on general wallpaper sites.
