Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WallDrop4K ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit walldrop4k.com (the "Site"). WallDrop4K is operated by Muhammad Saad. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We built this Site to be simple and free to use — browsing and downloading wallpapers does not require an account. This policy is here so you understand exactly what limited information is collected along the way, and why.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: automatically, as part of operating the Site, and voluntarily, when you choose to provide it.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, our servers and any third-party services we use (such as analytics or advertising providers) automatically collect certain technical information when you visit, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on the Site, and referring pages
  • General location data derived from your IP address (typically city or region level)

Voluntarily provided information

If you use our contact form, we collect the information you submit — typically your name, email address, subject, and message content — so that we can respond to your inquiry.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit. We use cookies, and allow certain third parties to use cookies, for a few different purposes:

  • Essential cookies — needed for basic Site functionality, such as remembering your cookie consent choice.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the Site, so we can improve it (see the Google Analytics section below).
  • Advertising cookies — used by third-party ad networks to serve relevant ads and measure their performance (see the Third-Party Advertising section below).

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Site, though core browsing and downloading will still work.

Third-Party Advertising

We may work with third-party advertising companies, including Google, to serve ads when you visit our Site. These companies may use information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We do not control the cookies placed by third-party advertising vendors, and this Privacy Policy does not cover their use of your information. Please review the individual privacy policies of any third-party ad networks for more detail.

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior information in an anonymous form. This data helps us identify which pages and categories are popular and where we can improve the browsing experience.

Google's use of data collected through Google Analytics is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data, and how to opt out, at Google's Privacy & Terms page.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its features
  • Understand how visitors browse and use different categories and wallpapers
  • Respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or unauthorized access
  • Serve relevant advertising through third-party ad networks, where applicable

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain automatically collected technical data (such as server logs and analytics data) only for as long as necessary to support the purposes described in this policy, typically no longer than 26 months. Information you submit through our contact form is retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry, after which it may be deleted.

Your Rights

GDPR (users in the European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Request deletion of your personal data

If you are a California resident, you have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information; however, certain advertising cookies described above may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of information under some interpretations of the CCPA, and you may opt out using the tools linked in the Third-Party Advertising section.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at walldrop4k@gmail.com.
