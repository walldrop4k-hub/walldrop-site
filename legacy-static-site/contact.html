Frequently asked questions

Are the wallpapers really free?

Yes. Every wallpaper on WallDrop4K can be downloaded for free, with no account, signup, or hidden fee required.

Can I use a wallpaper for commercial purposes?

No. Wallpapers on this site are made available for personal use only — as a desktop or lock screen background. Commercial use, resale, or redistribution isn't permitted unless you've received separate written permission. See our Terms of Use for the full license terms.

Can I submit my own wallpaper?

We're not currently set up for public submissions, but we're always open to hearing from photographers and designers directly — reach out through this page with the "Partnership" subject and a link to your work.

How do I file a copyright claim?

Visit our DMCA page for the full process. In short: email us at walldrop4k@gmail.com with the required information and we'll act on valid requests within 48 hours.

How often do new wallpapers get added?

New wallpapers go up on a weekly schedule. Check the "Latest drops" section on the homepage or follow a specific category to see what's new.
